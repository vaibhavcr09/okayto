Okayto started with a friend who couldn't eat wheat and a city full of kitchens that said “should be fine”. We decided to check, dish by dish, and write it down.

How a kitchen
gets verified.

01
A diner adds it

Someone who eats there sends the name, the dishes and a photo of the menu.

02
We visit

Within two weeks we eat there, ask about every component and look at the fryer.

03
Three diners confirm

Certificates only count when three diners photograph a current one.

04
It stays honest

Reports of cross-contact show on the kitchen's page until the kitchen fixes them.
